## **~~What is Dybuk?~~**

Dybuk is a minimal, distraction-free markdown editor for people who don’t want to learn markdown. Write like you’re using Notepad — headers, bold, lists, code blocks — all handled by simple shortcuts and a clean toolbar. No syntax. No clutter.

Perfect for scriptwriters, drafting notes, diary writers (who are afraid of someone reading their secrets), and anyone tired of Word’s bloat or Notepad’s blankness.

## **Why Dybuk?**

- **Zero syntax:** write naturally, markdown happens behind the scenes
- **Minimal UI:** one editor, one focus, nothing else
- **Encrypted vault:** lock your files in `.dybuk` format, sealed with AES-256 + Argon2
- **Local-first:** no accounts, no cloud, no one watching

> Once it’s sealed in the box, only your password can let it back out.

## The `.dybuk` Format

Your writing doesn’t have to stay in plain `.md`. Save it as `.dybuk` and it’s encrypted on disk, unreadable without your password. Not even you can get it back if you forget the password.
